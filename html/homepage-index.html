OUR STORY

WE TAKE
OUR TIME

HOT MESS wasn't born in a boardroom. It was born in a bubbling crock in a Sydney kitchen, where the chaos of natural fermentation met the precision of culinary craft.

For years, this sauce lived in home kitchens and on shared tables. Noa has been making it for friends and family for more than twenty years — slow-fermented, carefully balanced, never rushed.
